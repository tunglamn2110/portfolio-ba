HRM System – Hanoi Department of Health

Centralizing personnel data and standardizing HR administrative processes across healthcare units using an ERP-based system (Odoo).

Project Overview

The Hanoi Department of Health required a centralized HRM system to manage personnel data and administrative processes across multiple healthcare units. As a Business Analyst Intern, I supported requirement elicitation, analyzed current workflows, and contributed to defining system specifications for an ERP-based HRM solution.

My Role
Business Analyst Intern
Timeline
Feb 2026 – Apr 2026

The Problem

  • Fragmented Personnel Data: Employee records were managed manually and inconsistently across departments.
  • Inefficient Administrative Processes: Rewards, disciplinary actions, and appointment workflows lacked standardization.
  • Limited Data Visibility: Difficulty in tracking workforce information and generating consolidated reports.

The Process (Solution)

Requirement Elicitation & Analysis

Participated in stakeholder meetings with the Department of Health to gather and clarify business requirements related to HR administration.

Data & Functional Specification

Defined detailed data fields, relationships, and functional requirements for modules such as employee information, rewards & disciplinary tracking, and appointment management.

Testing Support

Created test cases and supported validation of system functionalities to ensure alignment with business requirements.
