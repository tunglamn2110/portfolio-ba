Odoo-based HRM System for Hanoi Department of Health

Supporting requirement analysis and functional documentation for a public-sector HRM system covering personnel records, innovation topics, appointment planning, and healthcare workforce reporting.

Project Overview

The Hanoi Department of Health HRM project focuses on digitizing and standardizing personnel-related data and workforce reporting across a complex public healthcare organization structure. As a Business Analyst Intern at BMS Technology, I supported stakeholder requirement clarification, functional documentation, field-level specification, and requirement validation for an Odoo-based HRM implementation.

Business Problem

  • Complex organization structure: Workforce information needs to be viewed across multiple healthcare unit types, specialties, and reporting levels.
  • Fragmented HR information: Personnel records, appointment planning, and innovation topic records require consistent data structures and field definitions.
  • Reporting difficulty: Workforce dashboards need clear indicators, dimensions, and comparison logic to support management-level decision making.
  • Requirement clarity: Business rules and data fields must be clearly documented before development and Odoo configuration handoff.

BA Process & My Contributions

1. Stakeholder Requirement Clarification

Participated in stakeholder meetings to clarify business requirements across 4 HRM functions: employee records, innovation topic management, personnel appointment and position planning, and workforce reporting.

2. Functional & Field-level Specification

Documented functional requirements and field-level specifications for employee profile and innovation topic modules, covering data structure, required fields, status tracking, document attachments, and search criteria.

3. Dashboard Requirement Definition

Supported dashboard requirement definition across 4 workforce reporting dimensions: total headcount, professional structure by specialty, workforce distribution by unit type, and quota-versus-actual staffing comparison.

Dashboard logic clarified:

Headcount: total workforce by scope and unit grouping.
Specialty structure: doctors, nurses, midwives, technicians, and other groups.
Unit distribution: workforce split by public/private and healthcare unit types.
Quota vs actual: comparison between approved staffing quota and actual workforce.

4. Requirement Review & Handoff

Reviewed and validated documented requirements with stakeholders prior to development handoff to ensure accuracy, completeness, and alignment with business needs.
